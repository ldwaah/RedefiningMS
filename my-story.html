My Story

Late 2023 changed everything.

What started as strange symptoms became a diagnosis that forced me to rethink movement, identity, purpose, and the future.

Oddly, when I was finally diagnosed with MS, there was also a sense of relief.

At least with a diagnosis, I finally knew what I was facing.

That does not mean the journey suddenly became easy.

But I continue to push forward.

Not because I ignore the reality of MS, but because I refuse to let my diagnosis determine who I am.

For so long, not knowing what was happening to my body and the uncertainty was frightening.

Living without answers can allow fear to fill the gaps.

In many ways, the longer I have lived with MS, the harder certain days have become.

Some days my body challenges me more than ever.
The fatigue. The frustration. The constant adapting.

It can be exhausting physically and mentally.

If this journey helps even one person feel less alone, then it has meaning.

Redefining MS was created from that journey.

  • Father.
  • Mentor.
  • Educator.
  • Human first.

A final reflection

Beyond the diagnosis, there is still meaning. This is the deeper truth behind the journey.
