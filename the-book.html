The Book

When My Body Said No

When My Body Said No is a raw and honest reflection on life before, during, and after a Multiple Sclerosis diagnosis.

Through deeply personal experiences, Lloyd explores the invisible reality of living with MS, the uncertainty, fear, exhaustion, identity shifts, and emotional battles that often go unseen by the outside world. From football pitches and hospital waiting rooms to fatherhood, mentoring, and rebuilding purpose, the book documents the difficult transition between who he once was and who he is learning to become.

More than a story about illness, this is a book about resilience, mindset, identity, and finding meaning when life suddenly changes direction.

Written with honesty, vulnerability, and humanity, When My Body Said No aims to help others feel understood, less alone, and hopeful that diagnosis does not have to mean the end of who you are.
